Questions families ask

Is it only for people applying for a plan?

No. If your child already has a plan, you can add it and use the yearly review, outcomes and provision tracking straight away. You do not need to have gone through the 20-week process with the app.

Where is my child's information kept?

On your device. We do not keep your case, evidence or letters. We keep your email address and your subscription status so you can sign in. If you use plan reading, the pages are sent to be read and are not stored.

How does the free trial work?

You get every feature free for 3 days. You add a card to start, but nothing is taken today. Cancel before day 4 and you pay nothing. We email you the day before the first payment.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Yes. One price covers your whole family, with as many children as you like.

Is this legal advice?

No. EHCP Tracker is a case organiser. It tracks the timescales in the law and helps you keep a clear record. For free independent advice, contact IPSEA or your local SENDIASS.

About EHCP Tracker

EHCP Tracker is a case organiser, not legal advice. It tracks statutory timescales and helps you build a clear written record — it doesn't replace advice from a qualified adviser.

Statutory timescales are set by the Children and Families Act 2014 and the Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014. Letter wording last checked against published guidance: .
