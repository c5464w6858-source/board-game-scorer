EHCP Tracker (also called Advocate), by IncludeMe · Last updated 31 October 2026

1. What EHCP Tracker is, and is not

EHCP Tracker is an organiser. It helps you track the statutory timescales of the EHC needs assessment process in England, keep a record of evidence and contacts, and draft letters from templates. It also helps you keep your plan and run its yearly review. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified adviser. For free independent advice contact IPSEA or your local SENDIASS service.

2. Accuracy

Timescales and letters are based on the Children and Families Act 2014 and the Special Educational Needs and Disability Regulations 2014, as we understood them on the date shown in the app under Settings. Law and guidance change and your circumstances may differ. Always check dates and wording against official guidance and your local authority’s letters before relying on them. Every date EHCP Tracker shows is worked out from the dates you enter. If a letter from the council or the Tribunal gives a different date, work to the earlier one, and tell us at hello@includeme.uk if you think EHCP Tracker has made a mistake. Appeal deadlines in particular are strict, so if you are close to one, ask IPSEA or your SENDIASS service for advice straight away. Letter templates are starting points: read and edit them before you send them. EHCP Tracker does not guarantee any outcome in an assessment, review or tribunal.

Reading your plan from photos. If you choose this, the pages are read by an AI service and the result is a draft only. It can miss or misread text. You are responsible for checking it against your plan before relying on it. The yearly review guide follows the SEND Code of Practice and Regulations as we understood them on the date shown under Settings, and your local authority may do things differently or the law may change, so check dates against your letters.
