Privacy notice

EHCP Tracker (also called Advocate), by IncludeMe · Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version. Your child’s details, your plan, your evidence, notes and letters stay on your own device. We do not keep them. The one exception is if you choose to read your plan from a PDF or photos: once you agree, the pages are sent to an AI service to be turned into text, and are not stored. We keep only your email address, whether you started a trial or have a subscription, and, only if you switch reminders on, the deadline dates needed to send them. There is no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who we are

EHCP Tracker is an EHCP case organiser run by IncludeMe. For data protection law, IncludeMe is the “controller” of the personal data described in this notice. You can contact us at hello@includeme.uk.

2. What stays on your device

When you use EHCP Tracker you may enter your child’s name and date of birth, your local authority, key dates in the EHC process, evidence you log (titles, dates, notes and optional photos), contact logs, provision tracking, letters you write, and your own name and address.

This includes information about your child’s special educational needs, which is “special category data”. All of it is stored only in your browser’s storage on the device you use. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. That means:

  • we cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you if you clear your browser data, lose the device or switch device. Use Settings → Back up now regularly and keep the file somewhere safe;
  • Settings → Erase everything deletes it from the device. Backup files you create are your responsibility.

4. Who helps us run EHCP Tracker

2A. Reading your plan from a PDF or photos (optional)

EHCP Tracker can turn the pages of your child’s EHC plan into text, so you do not have to type or paste each section. This is optional: you can type or paste the sections yourself, and nothing else in the app needs it. It is part of the free trial and the full version.

  • What happens. If you choose it, you are asked to agree first. The pages you pick are made into pictures on your device and sent, a few at a time, to our server, which passes them to an AI service (Anthropic, through its API). It reads them and sends back the words, which are shown to you to check. Nothing is saved until you tick it.
  • What we keep. We do not store the pages or the text, and they are not kept in any log. We keep only a count of how many pages your account has read today and this month, to stop misuse. The text you save is stored on your device, like the rest of your case.
  • Why, and our legal basis. To turn your plan into text at your request. The plan contains special category data about your child, so we rely on your explicit consent, which you can decline.
  • Accuracy. The reading can make mistakes. It is a draft for you to check against your plan, not a replacement for it.
  • Outside the UK. The AI service may process the pages outside the UK, under the safeguards in its own terms.

These companies process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify hosts the app and stores the reminder records and sign-in codes.
  • Stripe processes payments and keeps your customer record.
  • Anthropic reads the pages of your plan and returns the text, only if you choose to read your plan from a PDF or photos. It receives the pages for that purpose only.
  • Resend delivers our emails (sign-in codes and any reminder emails you chose).
  • Google Fonts supplies the page typefaces, so Google receives your device’s IP address when the page loads. No case information is sent.
  • Your phone’s own notification service (for example Apple or Google) carries reminders to your phone if you switch them on.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, it should be under a recognised safeguard; see each provider’s own privacy policy for details.

5. Storage on your device

EHCP Tracker uses your browser’s local storage to keep your case and to keep you signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Children

EHCP Tracker is for parents and carers. Information about a child is entered by their parent or carer. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it, object to our use of it, and ask for a copy. You can withdraw consent for reminders at any time in Settings → Deadline reminders.

The quickest way to delete what we hold is Settings → Delete my account. It removes your Stripe customer record, your reminder record and any unused sign-in code, then clears the app on that device. Stripe keeps payment records it is legally required to keep. Deleting your account also cancels your subscription straight away, so you would need to subscribe again to use EHCP Tracker. Deleting your account also signs you out on every device. You can end all sign-ins at any time with Settings → Sign out on all devices.

You can also email hello@includeme.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes

If how EHCP Tracker works changes, we will update this notice and the date above.
