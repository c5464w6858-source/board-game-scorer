## Full description (use for both stores; Google limit 4000, Apple limit 4000)

Never lose track of an EHCP date again.

EHCP Tracker helps parents and carers in England keep on top of an Education, Health and Care Plan. Whether you are applying for a plan or already have one, it shows what has to happen next and when.

APPLYING FOR A PLAN
- The 20-week clock, with the statutory dates worked out from the date you asked for an assessment
- What each stage means and what to do when a deadline is missed
- Template letters, filled in from your case, for when the council is late

ALREADY HAVE A PLAN
- Your next yearly review date, worked out from when the plan was issued
- A step-by-step guide through the review, with the dates for the draft and final amended plan
- Your outcomes, ticked off as they are achieved
- A provision tracker: what Section F promises, and whether it is being delivered

KEEP EVERYTHING TOGETHER
- Evidence log: reports, letters and assessments in one indexed list, with photos
- Contact log: who you spoke to, when, and what they promised
- Photograph a letter or report and attach it to your case
- Read your EHC plan from a PDF or photos into the app, so you do not have to type it (optional, and you are asked before anything is sent)
- A printable chronology and case pack to take to meetings or to an adviser

YOUR PRIVACY
- Your child's details, evidence, notes and letters are stored on your device, not on our servers
- We keep only your email address and your subscription status
- No adverts and no tracking
- You can back up your case, erase it, or delete your account from inside the app

TO KNOW BEFORE YOU START
- EHCP Tracker is an organiser, not legal advice. For advice about your child's rights, contact IPSEA or your local SENDIASS service.
- Dates are calculated from the rules in the SEND Code of Practice and the SEND Regulations 2014 and depend on the dates you enter. Please check them against your own paperwork.
- Because your case is stored on your device, make a backup from Settings. We cannot recover it for you if you lose or reset your phone.
- You can look around a demo with made-up cases before you sign in.
