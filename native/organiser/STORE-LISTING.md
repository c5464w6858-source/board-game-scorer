## Full description (Google Play 4000 / Apple 4000)

Child Organiser is a free app for parents and carers. It keeps the letters, appointments, contacts and reports for your child in one place, and turns them into a one-page passport you can hand to anyone who needs it.

Photograph it. We file it.
Take a photo of an appointment letter, a school report or a prescription, or choose a PDF or a photo you already have. Child Organiser reads it and suggests the appointment, the contacts and what the report says your child can do. You check everything, tick what is right, and save. Nothing is saved until you say so. The reading can make mistakes, which is why you always check first. Or you can simply type things in yourself.

What you can keep
- A diary of appointments, with reminders by email if you want them
- Doctors, school and emergency contacts, with a tap to call
- Medicines and allergies
- A portfolio of what your child can do, is working on, enjoys and finds hard, from their reports
- Letters and documents kept with the passport
- A one-page passport and an emergency card to print, save as a PDF or share

Your child's details stay on your phone
What you enter about your child is kept on your device. It is only sent to us if you choose one of the optional features: reading a letter (it is sent to an AI service to read, with your agreement), a private copy so the same details appear on a new phone, or email reminders. You can switch these off and delete what we hold. If you share a passport, you choose what goes in it.

Good to know
- Free to use. There are no adverts.
- Made for parents and carers, not for children.
- You sign in with an IncludeMe account (the same account as the Activity Finder). You can create one in the app.
- It is not a medical record and does not give medical advice. Check medicines against the label or ask your pharmacist.
- If you delete the app or lose your phone, details that are only on the phone are lost, so make a backup or keep a private copy.

Built by IncludeMe to make life with appointments, letters and forms a little easier.
