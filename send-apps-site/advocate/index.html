The 20-week clock

Three deadlines the local authority must meet.

Once an EHC needs assessment is requested, the clock starts. Advocate counts down to each deadline for you.

6weeks

Decision to assess

The local authority must tell you whether it will carry out an EHC needs assessment.

16weeks

Draft plan

If an assessment goes ahead, a draft EHC plan should be issued for you to review.

20weeks

Final plan

The final EHC plan should be issued, setting out the support your child will receive.

Weeks 6, 16 and 20 are statutory deadlines under the SEND Regulations 2014. Advocate helps you organise and understand the process. It is not legal advice. Try it with your own dates in our free deadline calculator.

What’s inside

Everything for the process, in one place.

Deadlines and annual reviews

Each step is marked done or upcoming, with the due date and how many days are left. Add them to your phone calendar.

Phone reminders

A notification on your phone, and an email if you like, a week, three days and a day before each deadline, and on the day.

Model letters

Letters that cite the law, filled in from your case and grouped by where you are in the process. Record when you send each one.

Evidence

Log reports, letters and notes, with a photo of each document and its page number in your bundle.

Provision and contacts

Track the support your child is meant to receive, and keep a record of every call, email and meeting.

Case pack

One printable document, with page numbers, for an adviser, solicitor or tribunal: dates, chronology, evidence index, contact log and letters sent.

Plain-English guidance

Each stage explains what it means and what to do next, so you are not working it out alone.

Your case stays with you

Your child’s details and your evidence are kept on your own device, not on our servers. Back up in one tap.

Works on your phone

Questions

Good to know.

Is Advocate available yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon. We will publish how to get it on this page, and it may launch as a website first.

Who is it for?

Parents and carers going through the EHC needs assessment process in England, who want to keep track of deadlines and paperwork.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, a 3-day trial so you can look around before you pay. It covers 1 child, up to 3 pieces of evidence and 3 contact logs, and you can view every screen. Letters, printing the chronology and case pack, calendar export and deadline reminders are part of the full version. After 3 days the app becomes read-only unless you unlock it, and you can always export your data.

Where is my child’s information kept?

On your own device. Your child’s details, evidence, notes and letters are stored in your browser on the phone or computer you use, and are never sent to us. That is why Advocate has a one-tap backup. We keep only your email address and what you have paid for, and, if you switch reminders on, the dates of your deadlines. Read the privacy notice.

Do I need an account or a password?

You sign in with your email address. We send you a 6-digit code to type in, so there is no password to remember or lose.

Will it send reminders to my phone?

Yes, in the full version. You choose whether to get phone notifications, email, or both. On an iPhone or iPad you first add Advocate to your Home Screen, which the app walks you through. Reminders are optional and you can turn them off at any time.

Is there an app to download?

Not from an app store. Advocate is a website that you can add to your Home Screen so it opens like an app, on iPhone, Android or a computer.

Can I get a refund?

We do not offer refunds after purchase, which is why there is a free trial. This does not affect any legal rights you have, for example if the app is faulty.

How much will it cost?

A one-off payment, with no subscription: £8.99 for one child, £3.99 more for each extra child, and never more than £14.99 for a whole family. Advocate is not on sale yet.

Is this legal advice?

No. Advocate helps you organise your case and understand each stage. It is not legal advice.

Is the IncludeMe Activity & Therapy Finder affected?

No. The Activity & Therapy Finder is a separate app and stays free for families.
