Free for families

Everything about your child, in one place.

One place for the things every school, club and doctor asks. How they communicate, what helps, medicines, doctors and emergency contacts, plus their documents and appointments.

Open All About Me How it works

Free. Sign in with your IncludeMe account, the same email and password as the Activity Finder.

What it does

Fill it in once. Use it everywhere.

Print it, share it, or keep it ready for the next meeting.

A one-page passport

How they communicate, what helps and upsets them, sensory needs, food, sleep and staying safe, with an emergency summary at the top.

Medicines and doctors

Every medicine with dose and timing, allergies, the GP and the rest of the team, and a reminder when the list was last checked.

Documents and appointments

Keep reports, letters and photos of paperwork with the passport, and every appointment with a calendar file for your phone.

Choose what to share

Leave out the NHS number, medicines or contacts for a particular club or school before you print or send it.

An emergency card

A small printable card for your wallet or your child’s school bag with allergies, medicines and who to call.

One for each child

Add as many children as you need and switch between them in a tap.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. Sign in with your IncludeMe email and password (the same as the Activity Finder), or create a free account.
  2. Add your child by name and fill in what you want. Each section is optional.
  3. Print or share it. A shared link opens for the person you send it to without an account.

Privacy

Your child’s details stay with you.

  • Everything is saved on your own phone or computer. It is not uploaded to us.
  • Documents and photos stay on the device and are never included in a link or printout.
  • You decide what each printout, copy or link includes.
  • It is not a medical record. Check medicines against the label or your pharmacist.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. All About Me is free to use. You only need an IncludeMe account, which is also free.

Do I need a new account?

No. If you already use the Activity & Therapy Finder, sign in with the same email and password. Otherwise you can create an account in a minute.

Is there an app to download?

Not from an app store. It is a website that you can add to your Home Screen so it opens like an app, on iPhone, Android or a computer.

What if I lose my phone?

Your passports are kept on your device, so there is a backup option in the account menu. Save a backup now and then, and you can load it on any device.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. It helps you keep information in one place. Check medicines against the label or your pharmacist.

Ready to make one?

It takes a few minutes to start, and you can add more any time.
