All About Me passport

One page that tells a school, club, doctor or carer what they need to know about your child: how they communicate, what helps, their medicines and who looks after them.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Stays on your device
  • Print it or share it
This passport was shared with you. It is shown only in your browser. It is not saved anywhere unless you choose to save a copy.

Keep it safe. A shared link contains the passport itself, so anyone with the link can read it. Send it only to people you trust, not in a public post. Check medicines against the label or your pharmacist. This is not a medical record or medical advice.

Why a passport?

New teachers, clubs, respite carers and doctors all ask the same questions. A passport means you write it once and hand it over, so your child doesn't have to be explained from scratch every time.

  • Everything is saved only on this phone or computer, not on our servers.
  • Add a photo, medicines, doctors and emergency contacts.
  • Print it, save it as a PDF, or copy a link or plain text.
  • Make one for each child. Update it when things change.
  • Choose what to include before you print or share, and print a small emergency card for a wallet or school bag.
  • Add it to your home screen to use it like an app, even without a signal. On an iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
