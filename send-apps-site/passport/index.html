How it works

  1. Sign in with your IncludeMe email and password (the same as the Activity Finder), or create a free account.
  2. Add your child by name and fill in what you want. Each section is optional.
  3. Print or share it. A shared link opens for the person you send it to without an account.

Your child's details stay with you

  • Everything is saved on your own phone or computer. It is not uploaded to us.
  • Documents and photos stay on the device and are never included in a link or printout.
  • You decide what each printout, copy or link includes.
  • It is not a medical record. Check medicines against the label or your pharmacist.
