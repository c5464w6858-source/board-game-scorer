Know exactly where your child’s EHCP stands.

The EHCP process runs to a 20-week statutory timetable of decisions, documents and deadlines. Advocate keeps every thread organised, with plain-English guidance at each stage.

6weeks: decision to assess
16weeks: draft plan
20weeks: final plan
  • The 20-week clockSee each statutory deadline and how many days are left.
  • Step-by-step guidanceEvery stage is marked done or upcoming, with what it means and what to do next.
  • Reminders on your phoneA notification before each deadline, so nothing slips past.
  • Letters and a case packTemplate letters, and one printable pack for advisers and tribunals.
  • Evidence, contacts and documentsKeep reports, letters and key contacts with the case they belong to. Your child’s details stay on your device.
