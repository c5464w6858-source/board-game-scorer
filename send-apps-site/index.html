Advocate · Coming to app stores

Know exactly where your child’s EHCP stands.

The EHCP process runs to a 20-week statutory timetable of decisions, documents and deadlines. Advocate keeps every thread organised, with plain-English guidance at each stage.

  • The 20-week clockSee the statutory deadlines at weeks 6, 16 and 20, and how many days are left.
  • Step-by-step deadlinesEvery stage is marked done or upcoming, with what it means and what to do next.
  • Evidence, contacts and documentsKeep reports, letters and key contacts with the case they belong to.
