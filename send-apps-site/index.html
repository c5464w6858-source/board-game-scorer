Two apps, one mission

Everything a SEND family actually needs.

Finding the right support is hard enough. One tool helps you discover activities; the other helps you navigate the EHCP process. Both are free and built from lived experience.

IncludeMe

Live now

A searchable UK-wide directory of activities, therapies, holiday clubs and support groups for children with SEND. Browse on a map or in a list, filter by age and need, and connect with providers.

  • Activities, therapies and charities in one place
  • Map and list views, searchable by town or postcode
  • Filter by category, age (0–25) and need
Open IncludeMe ↗

Advocate

Coming to app stores

The EHCP process runs to a 20-week statutory timetable of decisions, documents and deadlines. Advocate keeps every thread organised, with rights guidance at each stage of the assessment.

  • Full case timeline with a deadline countdown
  • Document log for reports, letters and evidence
  • Private by design, with guidance at every stage

Advocate

Know exactly where your child’s EHCP stands.

From referral to final plan, Advocate tracks every deadline, meeting and document, so nothing slips through the cracks.

  • Case timelineEach stage is marked done, due or pending.
  • Document logKeep reports and letters with the case they belong to.

How it works

From searching to sorted, in minutes.

Search your area

Open IncludeMe and enter your town or postcode. See every activity, therapy and support group within reach on a map or as a list.

Filter by age, need and category

Use the chip filters to show only what is relevant. Each listing shows its age range and tags so you can judge fit quickly.

Track your EHCP with Advocate

Stay on top of the statutory process with a deadline countdown, a case timeline and a document log from referral to final plan.
