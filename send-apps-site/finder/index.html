Questions

Good to know.

How much does it cost?

The Finder is free for families. Prices of individual activities and therapies are set by their providers and shown on each listing where available.

Does it cover the whole UK?

Yes, it is a UK-wide directory, so enter any town or postcode to see what is near you.

Are the listings checked?

Providers are reviewed, so you know what you are looking at.

Do I need an account?

A free account lets you save favourites and come back to them.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers.
