Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

What this app is

All About Me is a free IncludeMe app. It helps you keep a one-page profile for your child, with medicines, doctors, appointments, documents and photos in one place.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter about your child (name, date of birth, how they communicate, medicines, allergies, professionals, school, contacts, appointments, documents and photos) is special category data. It is kept only in your browser's storage on the device you use. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. If you clear your browser data or lose the device, it is gone, so use the backup option in the account menu.

What we hold about your account

To sign in you use an IncludeMe account: your email address and a password. It is the same account as the Activity Finder. We hold the email address, the password (stored securely, never in readable form) and sign-in times, through our account provider Supabase. Confirmation and password emails are sent through Resend. We use this only to run your account.

Share links

If you copy a share link, the passport you chose to share is inside the link itself. The part after the # sign is not sent to our servers, so we cannot see it, but anyone you give the link to can read it. You choose what a link, printout or copied text includes. Photos, documents and appointments are never included in links.

Storage and cookies

The app uses your browser's local storage and file storage to keep your passports and keep you signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing hello@includeme.uk. You can delete everything on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Contact
