Delete Your IncludeMe Account

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your IncludeMe account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@includeme.uk from the email address linked to your account
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account", or just click the link above — it's pre-filled
  3. We'll confirm your identity if needed, then process the deletion
Request account deletion →

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Name, email, account detailsPermanently deleted
Favourites & saved filtersPermanently deleted
Push/email notification subscriptionsPermanently deleted
Reviews you've writtenRemoved, or anonymised if removing them would affect other users' ability to see genuine review history
Enquiry messages you've sentDeleted from our systems. Note: if a provider already replied to you by email directly (outside the app), we can't delete a copy that may exist in their own inbox
Listing data (if you're an organisation)Deleted, unless you'd prefer your listing stays live and unclaimed — just let us know in your request

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days. You'll get a confirmation email once it's done.

Note.

Some information may be retained briefly where we have a legal obligation to do so (for example, records needed for fraud prevention or legal compliance), but this is deleted as soon as that obligation no longer applies.

Questions?

Contact hello@includeme.uk — see our full Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and why.
