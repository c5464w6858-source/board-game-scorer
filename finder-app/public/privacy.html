Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what personal data IncludeMe collects, why, and what rights you have over it. We built IncludeMe for families of children with additional needs, so we've tried to be especially careful and clear here — please get in touch if anything is unclear.

A note on sensitive information.

If you write a review, send a message to a provider, or describe a child's needs anywhere in the app, that information may reveal details about a disability or health condition. Under UK GDPR this counts as "special category" data. We only use it to operate the feature you used it for (e.g. passing your message to the provider you contacted) — never for advertising, and never sold to third parties.

1. Who we are

IncludeMe is a directory of SEND activities, therapies and charities. For data protection purposes, IncludeMe is the "data controller" of the information described below. Contact us at hello@includeme.uk.

2. What we collect

DataWhen
Name & emailWhen you create an account
Account type (family / organisation)When you create an account

We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. Where information about a child appears (e.g. in an enquiry message), it's provided by the parent or carer using the app, not by the child.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account and remember your preferences
  • To pass your enquiry on to the provider you chose to contact
  • To display reviews and listing information to other users
  • To let organisations see anonymised view/save counts for their own listing
  • To improve the accuracy and usefulness of the directory

Our legal basis is generally your consent (for optional features like enquiries and reviews) or our legitimate interest in running a useful directory service (for things like basic usage counts).

4. Who we share it with

When you send an enquiry to a provider, we share what you submit (your message, contact details, anything you've written) with that organisation so they can respond. We use Supabase to host our database and authentication — they process data on our behalf under their own data processing terms. We do not sell personal data to third parties, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Account settings, which removes your profile; some content you've posted publicly (like a review) may be retained in anonymised form unless you ask us to remove it.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to: access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or export it. Email hello@includeme.uk and we'll respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.

7. Security

Accounts are protected by Supabase Auth (industry-standard hashed passwords, optional sign-in with Google/Apple). We restrict access to personal data so that, for example, only you can see your own enquiries, profile and notification settings.

8. Cookies & analytics

We don't currently use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. Basic, non-personal usage counts (like how many times a listing was viewed) are stored to help providers understand interest in their service.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: hello@includeme.uk.
