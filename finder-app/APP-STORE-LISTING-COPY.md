## ⚠️ Age rating — read this before you fill in either questionnaire

**Apple's age rating system changed substantially in 2025.** The old 4+/9+/
12+/17+ tiers were replaced with **4+, 9+, 13+, 16+, 18+** (12+ and 17+ were
removed), and Apple added new *mandatory* questions covering in-app
messaging/communication, user-generated content, and medical/wellness
topics. This means any older advice (including earlier drafts of this
project's own guide) saying "this will be 4+" is now out of date — answer
the live questionnaire in App Store Connect directly rather than assuming a
rating.

**What's actually relevant in IncludeMe's case, to think through honestly:**
- No violence, gambling, horror, or sexual content — none of the
  "objectionable content" categories apply.
- **Direct messaging between users exists** (families messaging providers
  via "Enquire," and the review/response feature). Apps with open user-to-
  user communication are usually rated higher than 4+ specifically *because*
  of the communication feature, regardless of how tame the actual content
  is — this is a separate question from content themes. Answer honestly that
  the app includes user-to-user communication.
- **User-generated content exists** (reviews, owner responses) — also a
  separate questionnaire item from content themes. The reporting/blocking
  system built into the app (see the Reports tab in the admin panel) is
  relevant here as a moderation safeguard you can honestly point to.
- The app references health, disability, and therapy topics throughout —
  this will likely trigger the "Medical or wellness topics" question. This
  is informational/directory content about real-world services, not medical
  advice given by the app itself, which is a meaningful distinction — but
  still worth answering that topic question accurately rather than assuming
  it doesn't apply.
- Given the messaging + UGC factors above, a realistic expectation is
  somewhere in the **9+ to 13+** range rather than 4+, though only the
  actual questionnaire (with its specific current wording) determines this
  — don't pre-commit to a number before going through it.

**Google Play's IARC questionnaire** works on broadly the same logic
(communication features and UGC raise the rating; pure content themes are
asked separately) but is a different system from Apple's — you'll need to
complete it separately and shouldn't assume the two stores land on identical
numbers.

## 📸 Screenshots checklist (content, not pixel sizes)

Both consoles validate exact pixel dimensions automatically on upload and
will tell you immediately if a size is wrong — so this list is about *what*
to capture, not exact px values (which can shift between OS versions; check
at upload time).

Capture from a real running build (the device/emulator, not this chat's
preview render) once you're in Xcode/Android Studio:

1. **Browse/search view** — the main activities list with a few real listings visible, ideally with the search bar and a filter chip or two showing
2. **Listing detail page** — a fully-fleshed-out listing (e.g. one with photos, accessibility tags, and a review) to show off the depth of information
3. **Accessibility filters** — the filter panel open, showing the accessibility options specifically (this is a genuine differentiator worth highlighting)
4. **Reviews on a listing** — showing the real-family-review angle
5. **My Listings / business view** — if you want to also attract providers, a shot of the "Get Featured" / business side
6. **Map view** (if applicable) — showing geographic spread of listings near a location

For each, a short caption overlay (most screenshot tools let you add this)
helps more than the raw screenshot alone — e.g. "Filter by accessibility
needs" rather than no caption at all. Keep captions short; both stores crop
aggressively on smaller devices.
